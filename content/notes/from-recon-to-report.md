## Framework

The strongest reports are not just lists of vulnerabilities. They tell a coherent story about exposure, impact, and remediation priority.

## Stages

1. Gather the attack surface
2. Validate likely weaknesses
3. Capture evidence cleanly
4. Explain impact in plain language
5. Prioritize fixes realistically

## Closing thought

Reporting is part of the craft. A strong cybersecurity portfolio should reflect that as clearly as the technical testing itself.
