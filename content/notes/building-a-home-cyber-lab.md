## Start small

A useful home lab does not need to be expensive. Start with a clear purpose, a few repeatable workflows, and enough documentation to reproduce what you learn.

## Practical components

- A primary workstation
- Virtual machines for isolated testing
- Notes for every experiment
- A habit of resetting and documenting environment changes

## Portfolio benefit

Documented lab work makes your growth visible and gives you stronger stories to tell in interviews.
