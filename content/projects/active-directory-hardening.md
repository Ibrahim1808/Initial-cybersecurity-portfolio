## Scope

This entry documents a portfolio-style security review of an internal Windows environment with a focus on privilege hygiene, legacy risk, and practical hardening recommendations.

## Key checks

- Review privileged group usage
- Check service account sprawl
- Look for outdated protocols or poor segmentation
- Propose remediation steps with business impact in mind

## Why it matters

Identity infrastructure remains one of the most common pathways to high-impact compromise. A strong portfolio should show not only attack knowledge, but also the ability to communicate defense.
