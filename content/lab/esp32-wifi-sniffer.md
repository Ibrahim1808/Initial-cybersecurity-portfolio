## Lab concept

This experiment focuses on using an ESP32 development board as part of a low-cost wireless research bench. The goal is not to replace professional gear, but to understand packet visibility, hardware constraints, and data collection workflow.

## Notes

- Document firmware setup carefully
- Separate lab-only experiments from real networks
- Track what metadata is visible versus what requires stronger tooling

## Takeaway

Even small hardware projects can show curiosity, rigor, and a strong lab mindset in a cybersecurity portfolio.
